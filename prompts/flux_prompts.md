# FLUX Image Prompts (6 scenes)

These are production-ready prompts for FLUX.1 (e.g. flux-dev / flux-pro). Each includes a
shared style suffix so the 6 images stay visually consistent as a series.

**Shared style suffix (append to every prompt):**
`cinematic, hyper-realistic, dramatic volumetric lighting, 35mm film still, shallow depth of field, moody color grade, ultra-detailed, 16:9`

---

### Scene 1 — The Dark Start
A lone silhouetted figure standing in a vast, empty dark plain at pre-dawn, only a thin line
of faint blue light on the horizon, fog rolling low across the ground, sense of isolation and
quiet uncertainty, looking forward into the darkness.

### Scene 2 — The Doubt
The same silhouetted figure now dwarfed beneath churning storm clouds, sheets of rain in the
distance, jagged lightning far on the horizon, wind whipping dust and debris, the figure
hunched slightly against the elements, scale emphasizing how small and overwhelmed they feel.

### Scene 3 — The Decision
Close-up three-quarter view of the figure's face and shoulders, head lifting upward, eyes
opening with quiet resolve, the first thin shaft of golden light breaking through a crack in
the clouds above and catching the side of their face, rain still falling but lighter now.

### Scene 4 — The Grind
Wide shot of the figure climbing a steep, rocky mountain trail at a determined pace, motion
blur on the legs and trailing dust, muscles tensed, head down and focused, switchback path
visible below, overcast sky beginning to brighten at the edges.

### Scene 5 — The Breakthrough
The figure emerging onto a high ridge as the storm clouds visibly part overhead, brilliant
warm sunlight pouring through the gap and flooding the landscape in gold, wet rock surfaces
reflecting the light, a rainbow faintly visible in the receding rain.

### Scene 6 — The Triumph
Wide low-angle shot of the figure standing atop a mountain summit at full sunrise, arms raised
in victory, vast golden-orange sky stretching to the horizon, clouds glowing pink and orange
below the peak, sense of scale, freedom, and earned triumph.
